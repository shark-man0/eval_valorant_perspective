# Self Death: structural role qualification and release decision

Status: **proposal; no formal output or production adoption**. This document
requests a specific confidence/qualification contract decision, not approval
to treat the current diagnostic predictions as qualified events.

Follow-up: the existing source-1 NCC reference was omitted from a later
single-alternate profile bank. Preserving that frozen reference alongside the
compact reference restores actual numeric victim support for R1/R2 without
this proposed contract change. Their qualification can proceed under the
existing numeric contract. The decision below remains relevant to other
geometry-only cases; it is not a prerequisite for the numeric path. The new
profile still needs its own qualification and negative regression.

## Evidence and the remaining contract gap

The current image reader can detect the recording-player highlight, distinguish
its killer/victim side using an independent separator, and temporally corroborate
a fresh self-referential Report. See `self_death_role_reader_progress.md` for the
separate frozen/regression runs, exposure limitations and original outcomes.

A transport-only audit of five current-source predictions (source-1 R1/R2 and
three source-2 episodes) verifies the original source SHA, frozen result hashes,
profile/assets, code and dependency bindings. **All five victim-role readings
have a geometric witness and no weapon-template NCC.** This is an intentional
absence of a numeric score, not evidence to replace with a default.

`SubjectDeathProbe` currently requires numeric confidence >=0.90 for every
subject, victim and transition observation. It therefore cannot accept those
geometric role witnesses. Converting their plate fraction or Boolean acceptance
into a numeric role confidence would misrepresent the measurement. Merely
collecting more successful geometric predictions does not solve this mismatch.

The new transport preserves this distinction: marker scores remain measured
marker scores; victim numeric confidence stays null; transition support comes
from the **actual onset frame**, not the later confirmation. Every transported
observation remains UNKNOWN/CANDIDATE. No qualification or Self token is issued.

An additional source-native test processes every selected frame in three
nonself/stale-report/post-death controls: 1,519 frames, zero death predictions,
zero source-blocked frames. The long cold-start control alone includes 1,261
frames and 118 retained victim-role readings from the old killfeed. They do not
produce a new death. This is useful bounded negative evidence, not a measured
whole-match false-positive rate or a complete qualification grant.

## Requested contract decision

Allow an **independently qualified structural role predicate** to meet the
victim-role prerequisite without inventing numeric role confidence.

The proposed formal producer would require all of:

1. Independently qualified current-frame recording-player binding with unchanged
   three-support marker NCC >=0.90 and color requirements, source/profile/code/
   dependency binding and the recording-owner semantics review.
2. A separately qualified structural separator/row-role predicate, retaining its
   actual ROI, geometry and plate measurements. Distinct competing separators,
   killer-only rows, uncertain roles or unsupported source/profile scope reject.
   Numeric role confidence remains absent unless a real NCC reader supplies it.
3. An actually observed self-referential Report onset plus the current native
   temporal contract. The optional generic header cannot open an onset. Every
   confirmation frame must have current accepted Report and victim evidence;
   missing support, contradiction, deadline expiry or a source break rejects.
4. A frozen, reviewed qualification bundle matching the exact source scope,
   assets, reader, dependencies and predicate version. Reference-fitting images
   and qualification images must be separated and exposure must be explicit.
   Known regression frames do not become a new blind cohort. Count episodes
   separately from correlated frames or pairing combinations.
5. Qualification controls for self/nonself roles, recorder-as-killer, persistent
   and reopened reports, spectator/respawn/menu context, duplicates and source
   discontinuity. Missing controls mean no release. Existing negative assertions
   remain unchanged. Passing a few bounded cases alone is not adoption.

The **event's numeric support** would be bounded by the minimum of its accepted,
actually contributing numeric source supports, including the original onset. An
unrecognized caption stays unrecognized; a separately accepted generic header
can support current Report presence only under the existing pending-onset rule.
A structural predicate contributes a required qualification condition, **no numeric
score**. It cannot increase marker, caption, OCR or event confidence. No
confidence of 1.0 is generated from geometry acceptance or a qualification file.
All existing numeric thresholds stay unchanged.

This requires an explicit separate qualified-predicate contract; it will not
silently relax `SubjectDeathProbe` or its existing tests. Until that contract
and its independent qualification are accepted, current formal output stays
disabled. The alternative is to retain numeric NCC as mandatory for the role
and develop/qualify source-derived weapon-template coverage instead. That work
also cannot use the plate fraction as a substitute NCC.

## Downstream compatibility and safety

- The existing legacy HUD Death route and ownership guards remain intact.
- Native `player_death` uses actor `player` only for a qualified event subject.
  It does not change HUD identity, view owner, primary state, HP, Armor, Ammo,
  Weapon, Alive, location or their confidence. Generic Report presence cannot
  identify the subject.
- Proposed provenance uses existing event attributes where the native Schema
  permits it. No public Schema or migration change is authorized by this
  proposal. Any needed extension must be separately reviewed first.
- The event retains actual onset PTS/time base and confirmation/evidence times.
  Its stable identity derives from the observed source/subject/onset, not GT,
  inferred Round numbers, rounded seconds or decoder-session randomness.
- Native lifecycle determines package association by onset. In particular,
  late confirmation must not move a pre-end Death into the next package.
  Unqualified lifecycle/unknown membership remains unqualified; no R2 end is
  inferred. Preparation and unrelated observations are unchanged.
- Save/resume must preserve the distinction between missing, candidate and
  qualified proof, reject mismatched fingerprints and suppress duplicate
  source episodes. A generic Boolean supplied by a caller is not authority.
- Pack/Trace attributes must retain source proof. No evaluator or GT change,
  timestamp correction or hidden event insertion is proposed.

## Why approval is requested

S-01 additional instruction section 10 requires an impact analysis and approval
before changing the meaning of confidence or identity qualification. The user
also explicitly limited the earlier Self Subject approval to development and
qualification, without authorizing unqualified release or production adoption.
This proposal makes the structural-versus-numeric role decision reviewable
before implementing a formal consumer. It does not ask to weaken a threshold,
approve a missing qualification report, merge main or enable unqualified events.
