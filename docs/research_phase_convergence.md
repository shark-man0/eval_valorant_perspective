## Source and evaluation distinctions

`match_001` is an Outplayed-extracted portion of a longer recording, with no edits
inside that portion according to the user. It is not an uncut full match.
Repeated rasters or an abrupt screen change do not independently establish
manual editing. They also do not waive source continuity/ownership checks.
EOF, a player's death, or persistent/reopened Combat Report is not Round End.
The [2026-10-16 visual review](match001_visual_review_100ms_20261016.md) remains
historical, exposed evidence. No new source inspection or independent GT is
implied by recovering its document.

Canonical adopted historical baseline is **23 PASS / 55 FAIL / 4 NE**.
#48 re-scores a different, unadopted archived candidate from **28/50/4** to
**30/49/3** under a different timing policy: two policy-induced transitions,
zero demonstrated recognition gains. Raw absent/present brackets and original
padding versus replacement ±0.1s must remain distinct; never double-pad,
round/snap source events to GT, or alter canonical criteria to increase PASS.
Current-main canonical targeted/sampled/Full is unmeasured. Formal Round End and
complete match-wide lifecycle are not established by this convergence.

Self Death stays **B / BLOCKED**, formal real Self Death **0**, confirmed
independent whole-model held-out episodes **0**. Reuse safety guards, source
proof and [the existing Self Death handoff](self_death_research_convergence.md),
not unqualified reader output. Count and killer/victim/damage/agent attributes
need separate acceptance. Unknown fields remain unknown.
