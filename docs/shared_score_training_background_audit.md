# Shared score training and background audit

## Problem and evidence

The accepted team-score transport exists, but confidence alone cannot authorize
identity-independent global snapshots or round boundaries. Its fixed ten-class
bank needed traceable training provenance and current-code negative replay.
This work supports lifecycle inputs and score snapshots; it does not solve the
29 round-package assertions or release events.

The existing score assets are byte-identical to reviewed, frozen training glyphs
in `timer-glyph-cohort-v2`. No holdout is used for fitting. Current Gaussian3x3
comparison at NCC0.90 meets the prior minimum of three distinct physical support
frames and 80% support for all ten digits. Digits6/7 have only three supporting
frames, so this is limited training support, not broad generalization proof.

## Change and tests

The readonly audit records original physical frame/pixel/glyph hashes and exact
asset ancestry. A separate diagnostic replays the fixed54 off-HUD background
crops from three physical source frames with unchanged current ally/enemy
core readers, original44x45 crop dimensions, all ten competitors, NCC0.90 and
margin0.04. Profile preprocessing is loaded unchanged. Plan/review, encoded
source/pixel hashes, assets and code fingerprint are checked, including terminal
verification. Both diagnostics exit0; focused RuffPASS. No production code,
profile, validation pack, assertion, threshold or admission change is made.

The crop protocol already extracts a score-sized reader input. Applying the
profile's enclosing-ROI subregion a second time would change that protocol;
this diagnostic intentionally tests the core reader directly.

## Conclusion and remaining blockers

Training asset provenance is established, and the current reader still rejects
the reviewed background challenges. These are previously exposed controls,
not newly independent holdout. They are outside the HUD and cannot establish
natural absence at nominal score fields, crop-localization safety, or temporal
UI qualification. No historical qualification is re-signed.

Together with the fixed source positive review, this supports further work on
a separately qualified global snapshot admission contract. That contract must
preserve unknown owned facts and independently validate its complete producer
and source-binding path; it cannot borrow player identity or turn raw reader
confidence into qualification. R1 start still needs joint temporal UI evidence,
and R1 end retains the separate result-reference/timing blockers. Windows native
execution remains unverified.
